My Utah hiking checklist

  1. Choose the view
    • Red-rock arches near Moab
    • Waterfalls in a canyon
    • Alpine lakes in the Wasatch
  2. Pack essentials
    • Extra water
    • Sun protection and layers
    • Downloaded map and charged phone
  3. Protect the trail
    • Stay on durable surfaces
    • Pack out every item
    • Respect wildlife and hikers

My rule: Check weather and trail conditions before leaving.
